NovaRestore is a Paper Minecraft plugin that captures a full snapshot of every player's inventory the moment they die, then routes restore requests through a structured Discord approval workflow. Staff review each request, approve or deny it directly in Discord, and the player's items are returned — all without digging through log files or guessing what was lost.

## Onboarding in Four Steps

Get NovaRestore running on your server right now by following these four steps. Each one links to the corresponding detailed guide.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the Jar">
    Download the NovaRestore jar and drop it into your server's `plugins/` folder. No additional dependencies are required. See [Installation](/installation) for the full checklist.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Discord">
    Pass your bot token as the JVM argument `-Ddiscord.token=YOUR_BOT_TOKEN` and set `discord.log-channel-id` in `plugins/NovaRestore/config.yml`. See [Quickstart](/quickstart) for the exact syntax.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Permissions">
    Grant staff the `rollback.view` permission and players the `rollback.request` permission using your permission plugin of choice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test the Workflow">
    Kill a player in creative mode, run `/invrollback <playerName>` to open the snapshot GUI, submit a restore request, and confirm the Discord embed appears in your log channel.
  </Step>
</Steps>
